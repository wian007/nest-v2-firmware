warn.title=Before you start
warn.1=This replaces the firmware of the thermostat. It then no longer works with the Nest or Google Home apps.

warn.3=Only the 2nd generation Nest Learning Thermostat (board J49, Display-2.x) is supported. On any other model this will fail or do damage.
warn.4=Make the backup. Without it there is no way back to the stock firmware.
warn.agree=I have read this and the disclaimer, and this is my own thermostat
windows=On Windows the first step needs the WinUSB driver once: start Zadig, choose "Options > List All Devices" while the thermostat is in its boot mode (step 2), select "OMAP3630" (USB ID 0451 D00E) and install WinUSB. Do the same later for "USB download gadget" (0451 DF10).

rom.title=2. Start the thermostat in its boot mode
rom.1=Take the thermostat off the wall and connect it to this computer with a USB cable that carries data.
rom.2=Click "Connect". A list opens; leave it open.
rom.3=Hold the ring pressed for 10 to 15 seconds, until the screen goes dark and the thermostat restarts. Keep it pressed.
rom.4=When "OMAP3630" appears in the list, select it and click "Connect" there. You can let go of the ring.
rom.button=Connect
rom.sending=Sending the loaders
rom.done=The thermostat starts the service kernel. Its screen shows text.

svc.1=Click "Connect to the service". A list opens; leave it open. A few seconds after the start "Gadget Serial" appears in it: select it and click "Connect" there.

svc.waiting=Waiting for the service to answer

dfu.reconnect=If nothing happens after the thermostat is done, click here and choose "USB download gadget" again
dfu.stage3=Writing the kernel
dfu.done=Installed. The thermostat starts our software.
